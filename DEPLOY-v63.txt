Thai Fashion Lenses v63 — Discount Price

New feature:
- Admin has optional Discount Price (AED).
- Discount must be lower than Regular Price.
- Customer product cards show regular price struck through, sale price, and auto-calculated % OFF.
- Product detail, recommendations, lens-care cards, wishlist and cart use/display discount pricing.
- Cart and WhatsApp subtotal use the discounted payable price.
- Products without a discount continue to show the normal price only.
- v62 total-product-count and v61 return-to-list-position behavior are retained.
