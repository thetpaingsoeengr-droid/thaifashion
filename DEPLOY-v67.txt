Thai Fashion Lenses v67 — Product Variants

New:
- Optional product variants in Admin.
- Each variant supports Colour, Size, Regular Price, Discount Price, Stock, Waiting Period, and up to 2 Cloudinary photos.
- Product detail lets customers choose Colour and Size.
- Variant selection updates price, stock, waiting period, gallery photos and cart data.
- Cart and WhatsApp order include selected Colour and Size.
- Existing products without variants continue working unchanged.
- Products with variants are sent to Product Detail before adding, so a customer must choose the exact variant.

Retained:
- v66.2 image visibility/security hardening
- v65 compact powers
- v64 category colour behavior
- v63 discount pricing
- v62 total product count
- v61 return-position restoration

Important:
- Firestore rules file in this package uses the exact authorized Admin UID.
- Variant feature is optional. Existing products do not need migration.
