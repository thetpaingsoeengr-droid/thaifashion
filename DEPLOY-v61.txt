Thai Fashion Lenses v61 — Restore Previous Product Listing Position

What changed:
- Product detail Back button now uses browser history when the customer came from this site.
- Before opening a product, the shop saves category, color, power, number of loaded products, and scroll position in sessionStorage.
- If the browser reloads the shop instead of restoring it from back/forward cache, Firestore reloads enough product batches and returns to the saved scroll position.
- Existing v60 domain, security, Cloudinary, wishlist, cart, SEO and performance features are retained.

Expected flow:
1. Browse/filter products and press Load More as needed.
2. Scroll to a product and open it.
3. Tap the top-left Back arrow.
4. Customer returns to the same filter/list depth and scroll position instead of the top of the home page.
